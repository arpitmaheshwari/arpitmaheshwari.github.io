# Survey D — field notes from 97 domains (corpus_part_ad, locomotive.ca → ponder.ai)

Method: headless Chrome at 1440×900, one screenshot per site; blank captures re-shot with 7s settle + scroll nudge. Every verdict below is from LOOKING at the rendered pixels, not metadata. CSV: `survey-d.csv` (one row per domain). Hero JPEGs saved only for ORIGINAL verdicts: `d-<domain>.jpg` (14).

## 1. Tallies

- **97 domains: 81 loaded, 16 hard-failed** (DNS dead ×3, SSL/cert errors ×3, timeouts ×5, Cloudflare/anti-bot walls ×4, platform-dead pages ×2 — readymag payment-down, Framer site-not-found).
- **Verdicts: 14 ORIGINAL · 30 solid · 7 template · 46 n/a** (n/a = failed to load, or loaded but painted a blank stage headless — a striking 30 of the loaded sites are fully JS/WebGL-gated and show literally nothing without a live GPU/interaction; that gating is itself now a mainstream pattern).
- **Grounds** (of the 51 judged): dark 26 · light 16 · color 9. Dark is no longer a statement; a *committed color field* (teal, cyan, sage, coral, lilac) is the rarer, more memorable move.
- **Type voices**: grotesk-caps 21 · quiet 14 · mono 7 · mixed 5 · giant-serif 4. The default 2025 voice is a giant neo-grotesk in caps; serif at display size now reads as the contrarian choice.

**Most-repeated template patterns, named precisely:**
1. **Dark-dev-tool kit**: near-black ground, one neon accent (green/teal), pill nav, gradient glow, logo wall, stat cards (modal.com, para.co, multiversx.com, nexis.network).
2. **Centered-claim hero**: giant grotesk two-line claim, two pill buttons (white + dark twin), "currently working with X" badge below (marckuiper.com, offsuit.app, ponder.ai, overpass.com).
3. **Loader-first identity**: full-screen wordmark/percent counter before any content — so universal it cost 30 sites their headless render (otherlife.xyz's "2/100", polecat's "0%", noomo's pixel-morph).
4. **Terminal/blueprint chrome**: mono caps, dashed rules, crosshairs, live clock + city telemetry, [BRACKETED] labels (playlist.studio, pacolui.com, modul.es, nexis.network, lucasporterbakker.com). Now a genre, no longer a signature.
5. **Video-stage-only portfolio**: logo + nav and a black/white void that is really a video reel (markclennon, opalcamera, oddcommon, microdot, ossa's siblings) — indistinguishable from one another until the video plays.

## 2. ORIGINAL standouts (hero JPEGs saved)

- **makingsoftware.com** — A web page art-directed as a printed reference manual: pixel bitmap display face, `FIG_001` margin captions, blueprint-blue isometric diagrams on paper white. The genre transplant (manual → website) is total, coherent, and unlike anything else in the shard.
- **nav.supply** — One colossal, voluptuous inverse serif word "navs" fills the whole first viewport, cropped at the edges; below it, a plain shop index. Proof that a single typographic object at obscene scale can be the entire brand.
- **madebyarticle.com** — Pale-blue grotesk wordmark set larger than the frame and deliberately cropped, answered by a small news-serif strapline and a live clock. The sans/serif duet plus crop-as-composition lifts it above the giant-wordmark crowd.
- **movestudio.site** — Grotesk "Move" top-left is answered diagonally by a giant display-serif "Folio" bottom-right, over a b/w mountain film; a hairline meta row (agency, city, clock) pins the middle. Two-voice typography as layout structure.
- **o-p-e-n.com** — The entire ground is an extreme macro photograph blurred beyond recognition (amber, lips); the only sharp objects are a letterspaced mono "Op e n" and two tiny nav labels. Blur used as material, not decoration.
- **ped.ro** — The whole bio paragraph ships pre-blurred; only pill-outlined tokens ("PEDRO", "RAYCAST") are legible, begging the cursor to reveal the rest. Progressive disclosure becomes the site's one joke, perfectly told.
- **oliverjeffers.com** — Giant hand-painted yellow brush lettering smeared across a film still of the artist's studio; even the MENU is hand-lettered. The artist's own hand replaces the typeface — identity no template can fake.
- **ossa.wine** — The letters O-S-S-A scatter down a black-and-white Tasmanian crag in a fading grid, like scree falling off the mountain; one red word ("Tasmania") is the only color. Place, product, and wordmark fused into one image.
- **pedenmunk.com** — Flat teal field, staggered safety-orange caps PEDEN/MUNK interlocking with a hard-shadowed tequila-bottle photo; corner meta labels (Photo / Film). Editorial-poster energy, zero chrome.
- **orion.tube** — CRT nostalgia done as a system: rainbow test-bar glow, condensed editorial serif headline, a glitch-faced iPad render, dot-matrix UI captions ("TAP TO HIDE CONTROLS"). Product marketing with a fully committed period aesthetic.
- **oryzo.ai** — The brand draws itself inside a fake vector editor: dashed selection circle, orange bezier handles, hairline guides on sage green. Design-tool chrome appears in many sites; here it is the narrative, not a garnish.
- **oliverdupuy.com** — One dusk-lit architectural photograph full-bleed; the only typography is tiny letterspaced serif caps split between the top and bottom frame edges. Restraint so severe it becomes the signature.
- **muda.co** — Pixel-bitmap wordmark, one bouncing ultramarine ball, and an exhibition credit — nothing else on the page. A single kinetic object carries the whole identity.
- **polecat.agency** — Flat cyan ground with black display caps whose letterforms show their construction joints (plotter nodes/dots inside the strokes); even the "0%" preloader is set in the system. Type-as-machine on an unapologetic color field.

## 3. What separates original from competent

The competent sites assemble proven parts — giant grotesk claim, dark ground, neon accent, pill buttons, mono telemetry — and every part is now recognizable as a part, so the whole reads as a genre entry rather than an author. The original sites each commit to ONE governing idea (a manual, a blur, a hand, a mountain that sheds letters, a tool drawing itself) and then let that idea dictate typography, color, layout, and even the loader, accepting real costs — illegibility, emptiness, a single color — that a template would never risk. In short: originality here is not more craft, it is a single opinion enforced everywhere, while competence is many good decisions that anyone else could have made.
