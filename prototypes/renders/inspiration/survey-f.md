# Survey F — shard /tmp/corpus_part_af (95 domains, 2026-09-02)

Method: headless Chrome, 1440×900, one hero screenshot per site + DOM probe (ground color, biggest heading's face/size/case, canvas/WebGL/video counts). Every loaded screenshot was looked at by eye. Verdicts are about the *visible first paint*; sites whose openers are WebGL/video worlds under-report in headless and are marked as such in the CSV rather than guessed at.

## 1. Tallies

- **Loads:** 83 yes / 12 no (dead DNS, TLS failures, one Vercel `DEPLOYMENT_NOT_FOUND`, one Framer "Site Not Found", one 403, one parked domain among the loaders).
- **Verdicts:** 14 ORIGINAL · 67 solid · 2 template (zbs.capital's teal-glow crypto-VC boilerplate; t-ko.space parked).
- **Ground:** dark 40 · light 33 · saturated color field 10. Dark is now the default costume; a flat *color* ground (magenta, chroma green, marigold, crimson, lemon) is the stronger differentiator in this shard.
- **Type voice:** quiet 35 · grotesk 15 · grotesk-caps 10 · mono 9 · mixed 8 · giant-serif 6. The interesting sites are almost all in `mixed` and `mono`.
- **Most-repeated template patterns, named precisely:**
  1. **The dark-SaaS uniform** — near-black ground, 60–80px centered grotesk promise, one neon accent (lime/violet/yellow), glowing product-UI screenshot below the fold: trigger.dev, wope.com, twingate, usedropshot, usetool.bar, unicorn.studio, daybox.
  2. **The Apple paragraph** — huge quiet sans/serif manifesto sentence + hardware/phone render on black: umbrel, usedaybox, tiempo, supercommon.
  3. **The preloader-counter ritual** — 0→100% counter or progress blocks as the entire first screen (therobot 00%, thomasmonavon 08%, utsubo 1%, ylliramadani 94/100, watson "0", yinger's blocks). So common it's now a genre marker, not a signature.
  4. **The masonry video wall** — dark grid of autoplaying case films with a rail nav (twicetwice, studiosentempo, thelinestudio).
  5. **The serif-on-dark prestige play** — hairline display serif over black/film (v7labs, thenetworkstate, yourmajesty does it best).

## 3. What separates original from competent

Competent sites in this shard pick a proven costume — dark ground, one neon accent, centered grotesk promise, glowing UI screenshot, percent preloader — and execute it cleanly, which is exactly why forty of them blur together. The original fourteen each commit to one governing conceit (a coupon, a blueprint, a chroma-key stage, a painted canvas, a ledger frame) and then let that conceit dictate every component down to the cookie notice, the loader, and the nav chrome. Originality here is not more decoration but stricter internal law: one saturated ground instead of a palette, one metaphor enforced everywhere, and type doing structural work (slabs, ledgers, tickers, hand-drawn chrome) rather than sitting politely in the center.
