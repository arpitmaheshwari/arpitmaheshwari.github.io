# The 580-site field survey — consolidated laws (2026-09-02)

Six surveyors, 580 domains from ten galleries/award archives, ~500 judged from
rendered pixels. 83 ORIGINAL verdicts (heroes saved beside this file).

## The laws of the original tier (unanimous across shards)
1. ONE governing conceit, drawn from OUTSIDE web design (a coupon, a manual,
   a milk carton, a dam gauge, a trading-card binder), enforced everywhere —
   down to the loader, the nav, the cookie notice.
2. The move is STRUCTURAL — it lives in what the grid/nav/headline IS.
   It cannot be lifted off as a skin.
3. The signature is LOAD-BEARING: remove it and the site's meaning collapses.
4. One ASYMMETRIC CONSTRAINT accepted at real cost (monochrome only, cropped
   glyphs, no images, one color field) — the felt risk reads as authorship.
5. A MATERIAL you could touch: scanned paper, bitmap, drafting hairlines,
   ink-bleed, carbon copy — not merely palette and weight.
6. FIRST-PERSON voice: a dated signature, a daily sentence — never category
   language ("we craft digital experiences").
7. Spend originality in EXACTLY ONE place; everything else stays quiet.

## The banned list (each seen 5+ times; a new theme must avoid all of them)
- The Linear costume / dark-SaaS uniform (near-black, neon accent, glow panel)
- The centered-claim hero with two pill CTAs
- The giant "Hi, I'm X" intro sentence with logo pills
- Loader-with-percent as first paint (cost 8-30 sites their headless render)
- Aurora gradient corners · local-time/weather chip · viewport-cropped wordmark
- The italic-serif pivot word inside a grotesk headline (crossed to mannerism —
  NOTE: the current ember hero uses this move)
- Terminal/blueprint mono-telemetry chrome ("now a genre, no longer a signature")
- Video-void heroes and masonry video walls · dither/grain as instant craft-signal

## Field facts
- Dark is the default costume (~50%). A committed COLOR FIELD is the rarer move.
- The memorable lights are cream/greige/butter/paper — pure #fff belongs to blanks.
- Mono is the annotation voice, not the display voice.
- Display-size serif is now the contrarian choice.
