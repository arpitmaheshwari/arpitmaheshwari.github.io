# Survey A — field notes from 97 sites (corpus_part_aa)

Method: headless Chrome at 1440×900, one hero screenshot per site (4s settle), a second
pass at 8s + scroll-nudge for the 54 sites that captured mid-preloader. I looked at every
capture. Caveat kept honest in the CSV: ~10 full-WebGL sites never paint in headless
Chrome ("blank at capture") — judged only on what was visible.

## 1. Tally

**Visited 97 · loaded 93 · failed 4** (avara.xyz Cloudflare gate, bonsai.fyi DNS,
bruno-simon.com timeout, chain-labs.design parked).

**Ground:** light 47 · dark 39 · color-field 7. Light wins, but the *memorable* lights are
not white — they are cream, greige, butter, paper. Pure #fff belongs mostly to the blanks.

**Type voice:** quiet/sans 47 · grotesk-caps 20 · mono 12 · mixed serif/sans 8 ·
giant-serif 6. The strongest signature of 2025–26 in this shard is **mono-as-voice**
(spec sheets, bracketed metadata, counters) and **deliberate face-mixing** (serif italics
swapped into grotesk headlines mid-word; one monogram set in three typefaces).

**Motion hints:** canvas/WebGL 21 · kinetic type 13 · 3D objects 10 · film grain 4.

**Verdicts:** ORIGINAL 14 · solid 70 · template 9.

**The template patterns a new theme must avoid** (named precisely — each seen 5+ times):
1. **The Big-Caps Agency Stack** — 3-line grotesk-caps manifesto ("BRAND YOURSELF
   INEVITABLE"), logo strip below, smiley/badge sticker, dark ground. (airborne, andagain,
   brandappart, bennettandclive…)
2. **The Dark-SaaS Spotlight** — near-black ground, gradient keyword in the headline,
   pill CTAs, floating product screenshot, customer-logo row. (betterstack, blitzit,
   capsule, authkit.)
3. **The Aurora-Corner Bio** — dark page, green/violet gradient blobs in the corners,
   plain centered bio. (aleksitappura, alxmurashko, cedric.)
4. **The Local-Time Header** — "MEL [AU] 1:09 AM" / "Paris, France 7:25 PM" chip. Seen
   six times in 97 sites; it now signals genre, not personality.
5. **The Preloader-with-Percent** — [0%], LOADING 25%, giant counter "0". A third of the
   shard hides its first paint behind one; the counter itself is now a cliché surface.
6. **The Viewport-Cropped Wordmark** — name so large the edges cut it (andagain, atio,
   clementgrellier, bymonolog, aim). Twice is a movement; five times is a default.

## 2. The ORIGINAL tier (14, hero JPEGs saved as a-<domain>.jpg)

- **capetowndamlevels.co.za** — Civic data treated as editorial design: a giant serif
  "76%", a *written daily headline* ("Thursday's rain has shrunk sharply overnight."), and
  an inverted-triangle dam chart with the 2018 DAY ZERO line annotated. The original move
  is voice + instrument: the page reads like a columnist and measures like a gauge.
- **ballet-de-marseille.com** — The menu IS the homepage: News/Activités/Artistes stacked
  as poster-size black grotesk filling the left half, with dashed-border archive panels
  and mono footnotes at right. Navigation, hero and identity are one object.
- **benjaminhoppe.co** — The portfolio is a scanned, tilted paper letter — typewritten,
  dated March 5 2026, signed, with the work "available upon request." Material metaphor
  (correspondence, not showcase) doubles as a screening device.
- **andrewtrousdale.com** — Work rendered as an interactive knowledge graph: hexagon
  nodes numbered 1–5 wire themes (Positive Psychology, Human Expression) to projects,
  with a small index card as the only conventional UI. The site's structure IS the
  designer's thesis about how his interests connect.
- **arrowdynamics.dev** — The site is a hardware synthesizer: grain-textured device
  panel, working knobs, speaker grille, gauge card, serif-italic product name on the
  "screen." Selling an animation library by making the page itself the instrument.
- **blocks.shawnlukas.com** — A monochrome museum of design explorations: an annotated
  "808 ANATOMY" schematic with numbered oscillator/gain callouts, EQ curves, type
  studies. The annotation layer — engineering-drawing captions on artwork — is the move.
- **bychudy.com** — The portfolio is a constellation word-map: dozens of project names
  scattered at apparently random positions across a white page, thumbnails surfacing
  between them. Browsing feels like reading a star chart, not a grid.
- **arcade.la** — Bitmap/pixel display face over mono spec-lists with arrow glyphs and
  "AVAILABLE 2027": a solo practice presented as a terminal readout. Total commitment to
  one low-res material across every text size.
- **atio.studio** — The wordmark crops at all four viewport edges and the page
  self-documents like a spec sheet ("Background-color / Black 000000") while a giant
  LOADING… sits mid-page. The site describes its own construction as content.
- **alche.studio** — A drafting table: hairline construction lines, dotted arcs and
  grid verticals slowly composing on black, with one whispered mono sentence. Restraint
  as spectacle — the blueprint never resolves into a picture.
- **aim.obys.agency** — Archive of Ukrainian AI modernism where the AIM letterforms are
  cropped into constructivist geometric blocks — letter as image — over museum-catalog
  index rows (E01: Anatol Petrytskiy…). Typography carries the historical argument.
- **billysweeney.com** — An audience switcher rewrites the bio: "For anyone / Recruiters /
  Design Directors / Product Designers / Product Managers / Engineers." One conceptual
  control replaces the whole about-page genre.
- **brandingthatslaps.com** — A cloud of small video thumbnails scattered in z-space
  around the giant anchor word "SLAPS." — chaotic collage held together by one
  typographic center of gravity, framed by bracketed mono UI and city clocks.
- **abhishekjha.me** — A grain-paper card floats over a sky photograph; a giant ellipse
  is die-cut through it and metallic calla lilies grow through the hole; condensed caps
  collide with script ("CRAFTING/designing"). Paper-collage materiality, not flat web.

Runners-up (original move, didn't make the 14): adrienlamy.fr (red comic playground with
DRAG/GRAB/PULL doodle instructions), clarissemichard.com (monogram set in three clashing
typefaces), buttermax.net (wordmark swallowed by a transparent gameboy), caffe.design
(walking eyeball-cup mascot as tour guide), benvenusa.com (violet slab + serif wine-region
lists), akaru.fr (poster split with numbered color-block mosaic).

## 3. What separates the original tier from the competent tier

The competent tier arranges good ingredients — big grotesk, dark ground, local time, a
loader percentage — while the original tier commits to one governing *conceit* (a letter,
a synthesizer, a star chart, a dam gauge) and lets that conceit dictate navigation,
typography and copy together, so no part could be swapped into another site. Second, the
originals have a *material*: scanned paper, bitmap pixels, drafting hairlines, film grain
— a texture you could touch — where the competent tier has only palette and weight.
Third, the originals speak in first person (a dated signature, a daily weather sentence,
"listen to me") while the competent tier speaks in category language ("we craft digital
experiences"), which is exactly why five perfectly polished dark-SaaS and big-caps-agency
pages blur into one memory and the letter, the gauge and the word-map do not.
