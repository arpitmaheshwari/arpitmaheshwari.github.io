# Survey E — field notes from 97 domains (corpus_part_ae: ponpon-mania.com → studiogruhl.com)

Method: headless Chrome at 1440×900, 4s settle + 1.5s pump, retry pass at 8s settle with scroll
nudge for blank captures (58 retried, most recovered). Every loaded hero was looked at, not just
probed.

## 1. Tallies

- **Loaded and judged: 91 / 97.** Hard failures 6: sami.marketing and shareio.app (timeouts),
  shift-a.design (DNS dead), simonpan.com (auth error), stephenkentjohnson.com (Cloudflare bot
  gate), st33p.com (Shopify store suspended). A further 8 loaded but stayed blank in headless —
  WebGL/loader-gated (portorocha, posterco.tv, reflektor.digital, robertborghesi.is,
  rogierdeboeve.com, samsy.ninja, stagecrew.studio, richardprescott's 000 counter). Loader-gating
  correlates strongly with award-chasing studios.
- **Verdicts: 14 ORIGINAL · ~65 solid · 12 template** (template = the formula plus nothing).
- **Ground: dark ~52%, light ~33%, saturated color field ~15%.** The color-field minority
  (bubblegum pink serious.business, taxi yellow ryanhaskins, orange-red rig.ai/sebastian-martinez,
  cobalt samsons.kr, oxblood smlxl) punches far above its weight for memorability.
- **Type voice: grotesk-caps ~31, quiet-sans ~34, giant-serif ~11, mono-led ~8, deliberate
  serif×grotesk mixes ~13.** Mono is almost always the *annotation* voice, not the display voice.

**Most-repeated template patterns, named precisely:**
1. **The dark-SaaS gradient formula** — near-black ground, 64-90px tight-tracked grotesk headline,
   one gradient or glow accent, glassy product screenshot, pill CTA (satellitor, spellar, shape,
   retool, seda, remotejobs). Instantly parseable, instantly forgettable.
2. **The awwwards loader-gate** — % counter or mascot intro before any content exists
  (rodeo.film 12%, radiance 068, remyshoots 100%, richardprescott 000). So common it now reads
  as a genre tic, and it costs real reach: 8 of 97 sites never showed a hero to a normal-speed
  crawler or a slow phone.
3. **The giant-statement portfolio** — one full-viewport grotesk sentence, work grid below
  (stellar.work, rekki, strv, romemichal). Solid when the sentence is sharp; template when it
  is "we craft digital experiences."
4. **Serif-italic accent word inside a grotesk headline** (slash.com, sarahzaheer, seda, robin-
  noguier) — 2025's default gesture for "we have taste," now near-saturation.
5. **Live-status garnish** — local clock, weather, "After Hours — Sleeping" chip (sarahzaheer,
  radiance, september.works, strangefamily). Charming, and spreading fast enough to stop being a
  signature by itself.

## 2. ORIGINAL standouts (hero JPEGs saved as e-<domain>.jpg)

- **pp-fragment.com** — The typeface being sold is the entire interface: the wordmark sets itself
  in three optical styles at once, overlapping and shattering into floating 3D glass glyphs.
  Product-as-theatre with zero supporting UI — total commitment to one idea.
- **redchurch.london** — Hand-drawn letters rendered as liquid chrome, floating in real sky
  footage over an ENTER gate. Graffiti materiality composited into photography, not illustration;
  it feels like an object that exists somewhere.
- **reedhilderbrand.com** — A landscape architect whose hero is a razor-sharp elliptical lens over
  a blurred aerial film, captioned with live site weather ("Sep 2025, 74°F · Calm"). The device IS
  the discipline: framed views of land, conditions included.
- **rayraylab.com** — Off-white studio page whose mega wordmark is smeared with CRT glitch while a
  "REC WED 19:36:53" timestamp runs in the corner: the homepage pretends to be a live recording
  session. Mono annotations + service ticker keep it legible under the noise.
- **rig.ai** — Scanline grain over a hot orange-red field, chunky black grotesk, angular cut-corner
  buttons, then a hard drop to black for "You don't own your AI. And you're being watched." The
  paranoia thesis of the product is carried entirely by the surface treatment.
- **robhemus.co.uk** — Pixel-shredded classical statue holding a guitar, angled award-ticker tapes
  slicing through in blue and yellow, a contact marquee. Collage energy where every element is a
  different resolution on purpose.
- **sageeast.com** — Amber flared display serif on black where photographs live *inside* the
  words — a cutout replaces a letter's counter, a figure sits in the O. Typography as gallery wall.
- **samsons.kr** — RGB-cobalt field with neon green "Welcome," edges colonized by cutout collage:
  koi, chrysanthemums, zebra, Jagger, world leaders. Maximalist chaos held together by a boxed
  hairline nav — the tension is the design.
- **seunghyuk.com** — The hero is a mind-map: skill labels (STRATEGY, AI, PRESENTATION) wired by
  hairlines to a single black square nucleus on paper grain, with faint self-descriptions
  (RELENTLESS, CTRL+Z, NIGHT OWL) hiding between spokes. A portfolio that diagrams its author.
- **softlabor.biz** — A Times-set publication where every glyph on the page renders in ink-bleed
  dissolve, like a risograph mid-decay on peach stock. One print-native effect applied with total
  consistency to become the whole identity.
- **stiff.madebybuzzworthy.com** — Comedy production co. as 1950s cartoon title card: cream and
  cherry vintage display on ink-navy paper, a chattering-teeth mascot standing in for the missing
  letters of "GO OD," a vertical WORK ticker. Art direction, copy and layout all telling one joke.
- **strangefamily.co** — A photoreal 3D milk carton rotating in a real sky — fake script, halftone
  missing-person portrait, nutrition-label body copy — under a plain Helvetica nav and tri-city
  clocks. A fictional artifact instead of a showreel.
- **stravagar.io** — Black-and-white underwater world: a clam opens over a pearl, hand-drawn
  logotype, and a draggable year dial ("2020 — Pandemic Delights") that scrubs through chapters.
  Portfolio as explorable film, with the restraint of monochrome keeping it art not demo.
- **studio.ai** — The landing page renders inside its own design-tool chrome: the h1 sits selected
  with drag handles, a live property bar shows its font and weight, a toolbar hangs left. The
  product demonstrates itself by existing.

## 3. What separates original from competent

The originals commit to **one world-building conceit and let it govern everything** — a recording
session, a decaying print, a milk carton, an editor selecting its own headline — where competent
sites assemble proven gestures (statement type, italic accent, status chip, loader) that each work
locally but add up to nobody in particular. Original signature moves are **load-bearing**: remove
reedhilderbrand's weather-captioned lens or stiff's chattering teeth and the site's meaning
collapses, whereas removing a template site's gradient glow changes nothing. And the originals
almost always pick **one asymmetric constraint** — monochrome only, serif only, collage only, one
color field — accepting a real cost in conventional polish to buy a surface no one else can wear.
