The Misses Behind the Patterns

If you want to know how I think before the call, this is the fastest way. Four essays, each opening on a real moment a product I worked on lost — or kept — a user’s trust. All four are about the same half-second: the pause before a professional acts on a correct answer. The Patterns library is the reference version of the same ideas: the do’s, don’ts, and demos. This is the story behind them.

A recommendation engine that beat the traders it was built for — and sat unused. Two weeks lost to the wrong suspect, and the handover change that moved adoption without touching the model.
